\section{Experimental Configuration}
\label{app:setup}

Every value below is taken from the task configuration files (\texttt{task*/config.py}) and the saved run metadata. All experiments use seed 6304.

\begin{table}[h]
  \caption{Task 1: inductive biases and feature representations.}
  \label{tab:setup_task1}
  \centering
  \small
  \begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.24\textwidth}p{0.72\textwidth}@{}}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Setting} & \textbf{Configuration} \\
    \midrule
    Data & STL-10 test set; class-balanced evaluation subset of 500 images (50 per class); $224\times224$ inputs. \\
    Backbones (frozen) & ResNet-50 (ImageNet-1k, \texttt{IMAGENET1K\_V2}), ViT-B/16 (ImageNet-1k), CLIP ViT-B/32. \\
    Decision methods & Linear head on each frozen backbone; CLIP zero-shot with prompt ``a photo of a \{\}.''. \\
    Head training & AdamW, lr $10^{-3}$, weight decay $10^{-4}$, batch 64, at most 50 epochs, early stopping patience 5, 20\% validation split. \\
    Colour interventions & Grayscale; hue rotation by $30^{\circ}$. \\
    Spatial interventions & Patch shuffle: $4\times4$ grid of $56\times56$ blocks, one fixed non-identity permutation per image. Translation: 8, 16, 32 px in four directions. \\
    Cue conflict & AdaIN style transfer, $\alpha=0.7$; five class pairs (airplane/bird, bird/car, monkey/truck, car/deer, bird/cat), both directions; 200 images retained after visual review. \\
    Metrics & Top-1 accuracy, prediction consistency, shape bias, coverage, texture rank, cosine stability $I_T$. \\
    Statistics & Paired McNemar tests with Holm correction; bootstrap intervals (shape bias), Wilson intervals (coverage). \\
    Representation plot & $L_2$-normalised features, PCA, then joint t-SNE per backbone (perplexity 30). \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

\begin{table}[h]
  \caption{Task 2: unsupervised domain adaptation on PACS.}
  \label{tab:setup_task2}
  \centering
  \small
  \begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.24\textwidth}p{0.72\textwidth}@{}}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Setting} & \textbf{Configuration} \\
    \midrule
    Data & PACS, 7 classes. Sources (labelled): Photo, Art Painting, Cartoon, each split 80/20 stratified into train/validation. Target: Sketch, unlabelled during training, evaluated once in full. \\
    Model & ResNet-18 (ImageNet, \texttt{IMAGENET1K\_V1}) with a seven-class head, fine-tuned end to end; resize 256, crop 224. \\
    Optimisation & AdamW, lr $10^{-4}$, weight decay $10^{-4}$, gradient clipping at norm 1.0, at most 30 epochs, early stopping patience 5. \\
    Batches & 8 images per source domain (24 source) plus 24 target images. \\
    Model selection & Mean source-validation macro-F1; no target labels. \\
    DAN & MMD weight $\lambda=1$; three RBF kernels with bandwidths $0.5, 1, 2\times$ the median pairwise squared distance. \\
    DANN & Domain discriminator (hidden 256, dropout 0.5), domain loss weight 1, gradient reversal up to $\alpha_{\max}=1$. \\
    CDAN & Class-conditioned domain discriminator with the same settings. \\
    Controlled study & DANN $\alpha_{\max}\in\{0.25, 0.5, 1\}$, all other settings fixed. \\
    Separability probe & Balanced logistic regression, $C=1$, 70/30 split; source vs.\ target (chance 50\%). \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

    \toprule
    \textbf{Setting} & \textbf{Configuration} \\
    \midrule

    ERM & The Task 2 Source-only checkpoint, reused unchanged. \\

    Model selection & Mean source-validation macro-F1. \\
    Controlled study & $\lambda_{\mathrm{DG}}\in\{0.1, 1, 10\}$, all other settings fixed. \\
    Separability probe & Multinomial logistic regression, $C=1$, 70/30 split over the three source domains (chance 33.3\%). \\
    Sharpness proxy & Loss increase after one normalised gradient-ascent step of radius 0.05 on a fixed validation batch of 32 images per source domain. \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
