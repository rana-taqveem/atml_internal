Conclusion
Across all four tasks, strong closed-set performance did not guarantee robustness under distribution shift or unknown classes. Successful generalization required preserving class semantics: moderate alignment and augmentation helped, while excessive alignment or unstable optimization caused negative transfer. Overall, no single measure—domain invariance, confidence, or local flatness—was sufficient to predict reliability, highlighting the need for multiple complementary evaluations beyond IID accuracy.
